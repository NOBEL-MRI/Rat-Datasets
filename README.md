# Rat-Datasets
Datasets used in the paper: Advanced 2D Segmentation of Glioblastoma, Brain Regions, and Stroke Lesions in Rat Models Using U-Net Deep Learning Architecture.

Consisting in:
- 28 healthy rats. This translates to 392 MRI images (T2 Map) with their corresponding masks, highlighting the hippocampus, neocortex, striatum, and the rest of the brain
- 33 rats with ischemic stroke. These images were acquired at different time points after lesion induction: 24 hours, 7 days, and 14 days later, resulting in a total of 462 T2-weighted MRI images with their corresponding masks, highlighting the stroke and the rest of the brain
- 50 rats with glioblastoma. Again, these images were acquired several days after the surgery: 7, 14, and 21 days. This resulted in 1,095 T2-weighted images and their corresponding masks, segmenting the tumor.
